## 1. Merchandising

### Official Workflow Addressed

BlueKaktus describes merchandising as the module for centralizing buyer enquiries, sampling, costing, orders, timelines, and BOM creation. The module is positioned to help merchandising teams move faster, stay aligned with buyers, and control costing, orders, and timelines.

Official capabilities include:

- Buyer enquiry management.
- Sampling workflow management.
- Costing control.
- Order management.
- TNA planning.
- BOM generation.

### What It Does

The module captures buyer enquiries with detailed information and converts them into sampling or costing workflows. It tracks sample lifecycle, buyer feedback, and approvals. It supports initial, budgeted, and actual costing and converts approved styles into production orders. It also builds milestone calendars using TNA planning and auto-generates BOMs to prevent material mismatches.

### Logic Used

Officially stated logic:

- Enquiry-to-sampling or enquiry-to-costing conversion.
- Sample lifecycle and approval tracking.
- Initial, budgeted, and actual costing comparison.
- Production order creation from approved styles.
- TNA milestone calendar creation.
- BOM generation.

Interpretation:

- The core logic is a stage-gated merchandising workflow. A style moves through enquiry, sample, costing, approval, order, TNA, and BOM readiness states.
- Costing likely depends on style attributes, BOM consumption, trims, fabric, CM, overhead, margin, buyer terms, vendor/factory rates, and currency assumptions.
- TNA dates are usually generated by templates tied to order date, delivery date, shipment date, or ex-factory date.
- BOM generation links technical product data to procurement and production readiness.

### Promise of Delivery

BlueKaktus claims 30% faster order conversion, 25% faster sampling timelines, and real-time margin visibility on the manufacturing overview page. The merchandising page also emphasizes faster go-to-market, full visibility into costing and margins, and real-time collaboration.

### What It Means in System Use

Using the module means merchandisers stop keeping buyer communication, sampling progress, costing sheets, and TNA calendars in separate files. The system becomes the operational control point for:

- Buyer enquiry records.
- Style and sample status.
- Costing versions.
- Buyer feedback and approvals.
- Production order conversion.
- TNA milestone ownership.
- BOM handoff to procurement and production.

### Tech Engagement

Likely implementation work:

- Configure enquiry, sample, costing, and order forms.
- Load buyer, style, material, costing, and sample masters.
- Define TNA templates.
- Configure approval roles and escalation rules.
- Integrate BOM output with procurement and production planning modules.

## 2. Procurement

### Official Workflow Addressed

BlueKaktus positions procurement as an end-to-end material and vendor control module. It covers vendor POs, job work, GRN automation, inspections, stock reports, valuation, and traceability.

Official capabilities include:

- Purchase order management.
- Job work order tracking.
- GRN automation.
- Inspection and quality validation.
- Stock reports and valuation.
- Integration with inventory, finance, and production.

### What It Does

The procurement module creates, shares, and manages purchase orders digitally, with auto-linking to costing and BOM. It monitors job-work deliveries, consumption, and returns. It auto-generates GRNs on receipt and validates them against POs. It defines QC parameters at PO creation and tracks approvals before stock entry. It provides real-time stock reports and valuation for raw materials and job work.

### Logic Used

Officially stated logic:

- BOM and order specs are linked to POs.
- GRNs are validated against POs.
- QC parameters can be defined before receipt.
- Stock and valuation update from procurement events.

Interpretation:

- This is a material-readiness and traceability workflow. The planning value comes from knowing which ordered materials are expected, received, inspected, approved, rejected, issued, consumed, or returned.
- Logic likely includes PO status tracking, quantity tolerance checks, delivery schedule checks, GRN matching, QC gate pass/fail, lot/batch tracking, and stock valuation calculations.
- Integration with finance implies a three-way or multi-way matching concept: PO, GRN/QC, vendor invoice, and payment workflow.

### Promise of Delivery

BlueKaktus claims 35% faster PO processing, 100% GRN accuracy, full material traceability, fewer GRN/QC errors, improved supplier collaboration, and reduced material wastage and cost overruns.

### What It Means in System Use

Procurement users would manage material commitments and receipts inside BlueKaktus rather than through disconnected PO files and receipt registers. Factory planners can see whether production can actually start because material readiness, QC approval, and stock availability are visible.

### Tech Engagement

Likely implementation work:

- Map material masters, vendors, UOMs, tax structures, warehouse locations, and quality parameters.
- Configure PO templates, approval rules, job-work flows, and GRN validation.
- Define inventory valuation rules.
- Integrate procurement events with production plans and accounting postings.

## 3. Production Planning

### Official Workflow Addressed

BlueKaktus describes production planning as the module that optimizes production operations from routing to WIP valuation. It is intended to help production teams execute, reduce bottlenecks, and control processes, materials, and performance.

Official capabilities include:

- Production process routing.
- SAM definition.
- Dye lot and lot cut planning.
- Daily production tracking and reports.
- Raw material consumption.
- WIP valuation.
- End-to-end production control.

### What It Does

The module lets users define and manage production processes, set SAM, plan dye lots and lot cutting, track daily output, monitor raw-material consumption, and value WIP. It gives real-time visibility into production activity, semi-finished goods, material usage, and costing implications.

### Logic Used

Officially stated logic:

- Routing defines production workflow steps.
- SAM is assigned to processes.
- Dye-lot and lot-cut planning optimize fabric use.
- Daily outputs feed production reports.
- Raw-material consumption is monitored against production orders.
- WIP valuation tracks semi-finished goods.

Interpretation:

- Routing and SAM logic create the standard time and sequence baseline for production planning.
- Lot-cut planning likely uses fabric availability, color/dye-lot grouping, size ratios, order quantity, marker/cut efficiency, and wastage allowances.
- WIP valuation usually combines issued material, labor/process completion, production stage, and cost accumulation.
- Daily production reporting feeds variance analysis, efficiency, bottleneck detection, and capacity planning.

### Promise of Delivery

BlueKaktus claims 20% faster production execution, 75% reduction in material wastage, better capacity utilization, full WIP visibility, audit-ready production reports, and reduced material wastage and cost overruns.

### What It Means in System Use

Production planning users would use BlueKaktus to define how an order will move through the factory and how material will be consumed. It becomes the link between order readiness, cutting/lot planning, production execution, WIP valuation, and costing.

### Tech Engagement

Likely implementation work:

- Configure operation routes, production stages, SAM libraries, style-process templates, production locations, and WIP valuation rules.
- Define cut planning and lot planning data.
- Connect production reporting with MES, inventory, and finance.
- Train production, IE, stores, and finance users on shared operational records.

## 4. Shipping Documentation

### Official Workflow Addressed

BlueKaktus positions shipping documentation as an export and dispatch automation module. It covers commercial invoices, packing lists, custom documentation, dispatch and carrier notes, post-shipment tracking, and incentive claims.

Official capabilities include:

- Auto-generated invoices and commercial bills.
- Packing list and custom documentation.
- Dispatch and carrier note automation.
- Post-shipment tracking.
- Incentive claim tracking.
- Centralized shipping dashboard.
- ERP integration and audit-ready documentation.

### What It Does

The module pulls data from orders to create standardized export and shipping documents. It digitizes packing lists, compliance forms, certificates, dispatch notes, carrier delivery notes, post-shipment status, and export incentive claims such as RoDTEP, MEIS, duty drawbacks, and other recoveries.

### Logic Used

Officially stated logic:

- Documents are created from order data.
- Shipment status can be monitored through tracking integrations.
- Incentive claims are tracked.
- Documents carry timestamps and version control.

Interpretation:

- This is order-to-dispatch and dispatch-to-cash control.
- Logic likely includes document template generation, shipment packing validation, invoice line mapping, carton and size/color packing structure, export compliance field validation, dispatch status, carrier tracking, and claim status.

### Promise of Delivery

BlueKaktus claims 50% faster documentation turnaround, zero manual invoice errors, full shipment traceability, and audit-ready documentation.

### What It Means in System Use

Export and logistics users would generate documentation from approved operational data rather than retyping values into external templates. Finance and compliance teams get a shared document trail.

### Tech Engagement

Likely implementation work:

- Configure document templates, buyer/export formats, invoice rules, packing structures, carrier fields, and statutory/incentive fields.
- Integrate shipment tracking where available.
- Connect order, inventory, finance, and shipping data.

## 5. Finance and Accounting

### Official Workflow Addressed

BlueKaktus describes finance/accounting as integrated financial control tied to purchases, job work, production, and reporting.

Official capabilities include:

- Purchase, job work, and production linkage.
- Bill passing and payment workflows.
- Debit notes, credit notes, and vouchers.
- Automated trial balance, P&L, and balance sheet.
- Operational integration with production, sourcing, and inventory.

### What It Does

The module automatically creates vouchers from POs, job-work challans, and production entries. It digitizes bill approvals with PO/GRN verification. It generates and tracks debit notes, credit notes, vouchers, trial balance, P&L, and balance sheets.

### Logic Used

Officially stated logic:

- Financial transactions are linked to operational events.
- Invoices are auto-validated against purchase and production data.
- Reports are generated with drill-downs.

Interpretation:

- This module is designed to reduce post-facto accounting reconstruction. Financial entries arise from procurement, job work, production, inventory, and shipping events.
- Logic likely includes account mapping, ledger posting, tax/charge handling, invoice verification, debit/credit note workflows, payment approval, and reporting aggregation.

### Promise of Delivery

BlueKaktus claims 40% faster bill approvals, 100% transparency in adjustments, real-time financial reporting, error-free entries, and audit-ready compliance.

### What It Means in System Use

Finance users work with operationally linked transactions. Instead of separately asking procurement, stores, job-work, and production teams for evidence, finance can drill into the source documents and approvals.

### Tech Engagement

Likely implementation work:

- Configure chart of accounts, cost centers, vendor ledgers, taxes, voucher types, approval workflows, and reporting formats.
- Map operational events to accounting postings.
- Define controls for bill passing, adjustment notes, and audit logs.

## 6. Manufacturer ERP Synthesis

The manufacturer ERP story is strongest when these modules are connected:

- Merchandising creates the commercial and style/order baseline.
- BOM and TNA create the material and timeline baseline.
- Procurement turns BOM requirements into vendor commitments and material readiness.
- Production planning turns orders and materials into routes, lots, outputs, consumption, and WIP.
- Shipping turns completed orders into dispatch documentation and traceability.
- Finance turns operational events into controlled accounting and reporting.

Interpretation: BlueKaktus' ERP value proposition is not any one module alone. The value is the continuity of data from enquiry to dispatch and from operational event to financial control.
