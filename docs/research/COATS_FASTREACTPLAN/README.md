# Coats Digital FastReactPlan Research Notes

Date prepared: 2026-06-03

This folder documents the public FastReactPlan offering from Coats Digital. It follows the same research stance used for the BlueKaktus notes: official-source led, capability-by-capability, and explicit about where apparel planning interpretation has been added.

FastReactPlan is positioned by Coats Digital as a market-leading production planning and control solution for apparel, footwear, textile, and fashion manufacturers. Its core claim is dynamic planning of three linked constraints:

- Capacity.
- Materials.
- Pre-production critical path.

The public product story is not a broad ERP story. FastReactPlan is a specialist planning and control layer that connects with ERP, PLM, shop-floor data collection, and other business systems.

## Evidence Stance

The documents use three evidence labels:

- Official: directly stated on a Coats Digital page.
- Officially supported: supported by official page relationships, customer case studies, or repeated Coats Digital descriptions.
- Interpretation: generic apparel planning, production control, MRP, critical path, APS, or integration context added to explain how the capability likely works operationally.

## Key Research Caveat

Coats Digital publishes strong capability descriptions and many customer outcome claims, but it does not disclose its full internal data model, scheduling algorithms, deployment architecture, security model, or configuration schema on the public site. Those areas are treated as evaluation probes rather than assumed facts.
